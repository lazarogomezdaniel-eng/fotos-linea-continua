## 1. Overview & Core Privacy Promise
LolyLine is an offline-first creative art application designed to convert portraits and photos into single continuous line art drawings.

**Our Core Promise:**  
We believe your personal photos are private. **All image processing, computer vision algorithms, and vector line generation happen 100% locally on your device.** Your photos and artwork never leave your phone or tablet. We do not operate remote image processing servers, and we never collect, transmit, or share your images.

---

## 2. Permissions Requested & How They Are Used

### 2.1 Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`)
- **Purpose:** Used solely when you choose the "Take Photo" (Tomar Foto) feature to capture a picture of yourself, a pet, or an object to turn into line art.
- **Data Handling:** The camera stream and captured photos are processed entirely in the local memory of your device. Photos are neither uploaded nor stored externally.

### 2.2 Filesystem & Scoped Storage (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / Media Store)
- **Purpose:** Used solely when you select "Gallery" (Galería) to pick an existing image from your device, or when you choose to export your generated artwork (PNG, SVG, or WebM time-lapse video).
- **Data Handling:** Files are saved directly to your device's storage or shared via Android's native system share sheet. We have no access to files outside of those you explicitly select or export.

## 3. In-App Purchases & Financial Information
- LolyLine offers an optional one-time purchase ("LolyLine Pro") to unlock SVG vector export, watermark removal, and Ultra HD resolutions.
- All transactions are handled exclusively by **Google Play Billing** (Google LLC) or **Amazon In-App Purchasing** (Amazon.com, Inc.).
- LolyLine never accesses, processes, or stores your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or payment credentials.

---

## 4. Analytics, Tracking & Advertisements
- LolyLine **does not** contain any advertising SDKs (no AdMob, no Unity Ads, etc.).
- LolyLine **does not** contain third-party behavioural tracking or data broker analytics.
- LolyLine operates fully in Airplane Mode (offline).

## 5. Children's Privacy
LolyLine does not collect any personal information from children or adults. It is completely safe for all audiences and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
